Superintelligence will not arrive by scale alone.

Larger models keep improving on benchmarks while generality stalls. Their behaviour cannot be specified before they run, checked while they run, or bounded afterwards. The capability problem and the control problem are the same problem.

Progress from here depends on control. A system whose output can be checked is a system that can be searched against that check, and search against a reliable check is where superhuman performance has actually come from.

We are building that construction deliberately rather than stumbling into it: learned models placed under search, verification and proof. Guarantees are deliverables, not evaluations run afterwards.

We are a small team working on one programme. There is nothing to announce yet.
